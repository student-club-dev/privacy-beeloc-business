Frequently asked questions

I did not receive the SMS code

Check that the number has 9 digits after +998 and that your phone has signal. Wait for the timer to finish and tap "Resend code". If it still does not arrive, write to us with your phone number.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen tap "Forgot password?", enter your phone number, then the SMS code, and choose a new password.

Why is my business or listing "In review"?

Every business and listing is checked by our team before it is shown to students. This normally takes a short time. If it is rejected, the reason is shown on the business or listing so you can fix it and submit again.

How do I add a branch or change working hours?

Open My businesses, choose your business and tap Edit. There you can add branches, pick the location on the map and set working hours for each day.

How does a customer redeem a discount?

The customer shows a code. Open the redeem screen in the app, enter the code, tap Verify and then Confirm.

How do I pay for the subscription or turn off auto-payment?

In Billing and subscription, choose a period and pay. Turn off auto-renewal with the "Active" switch on the My businesses screen. See the Payment and Refund Policy for details.

The map does not load

The map needs an internet connection. Check your connection and open the screen again.

Delete my account and data

In the app: Settings → Delete account. An SMS code is sent to your account's verified number; after you enter it, your account and all related data (profile, businesses, listings, job postings, photos, messages, saved cards) are deleted.

If you cannot sign in, email quvonchbekgafurov07@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" and the account's phone number (with +998). We may ask you to confirm with an SMS code. Data is deleted or anonymised within 30 days; records the law requires us to keep are retained for the required period.

Deletion cannot be undone.
