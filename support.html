Frequently asked questions

I did not receive the SMS code

Check that the number is typed with 9 digits after +998 and that your phone has signal. Wait for the timer to finish and tap "Resend code". If it still does not arrive, write to support with your phone number.

I forgot my password

On the sign-in screen tap "Forgot password?", enter your phone number, then enter the SMS code and choose a new password.

Why is my business or listing "In review"?

Every business and listing is checked by our team before it becomes visible to end users. This normally takes a short time. If it is rejected, the reason is shown on the business or listing so that you can fix it and submit it again.

How do I add a branch or change working hours?

Open My businesses, choose your business and tap Edit. There you can add branches, pick the location on the map and set working hours for each day.

How does a customer redeem a discount?

The customer shows a code. Open the redeem screen in the app, enter the code, tap Verify and then Confirm.

The map does not load

The map needs an internet connection. Check your connection and open the screen again.

Delete my account and data

You can ask us to permanently delete your account and the data linked to it (profile, businesses, listings, job postings, photos and messages).

  1. Send an email to quvonchbekgafurov07@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Beeloc Business account".
  2. Write the phone number of the account (with +998). We may ask you to confirm with an SMS code.
  3. We delete or anonymize your data within 30 days and confirm by email. Records that the law requires us to keep are retained for the legally required period.

Deletion cannot be undone.
