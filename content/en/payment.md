## 2. What the subscription includes

A subscription is taken out separately for each business. While it is active, your business's listings are shown in the student app and subscription features are available. If the subscription ends and is not renewed, these features are paused; your business and listings are not deleted and come back when the subscription is reactivated.

## 3. Price

3.1. The price is based on the monthly rate for your business type and the number of branches your business has. The exact amount is shown before payment on the **Billing and subscription** screen in the App.

3.2. You can prepay for 1, 3, 6 or 12 months. The amount equals the monthly price multiplied by the number of months.

3.3. The price is fixed at the time of payment and does not change during the paid period. Rate changes are announced in the App at least 10 days in advance and apply from the next payment.

3.4. Prices are in Uzbek soum. The payment provider's fee is not charged to you separately.

## 4. Payment methods

Payments are accepted through **Atmos**, a licensed payment institution. Available methods:

- **Uzcard** and **Humo** cards;
- **Click**, **Payme** and **Uzum** payment apps.

You pay on Atmos's secure page or with a card linked in the App. Card details are processed by Atmos; we do not store your full card number or CVV.

## 5. Auto-renewal

5.1. If you link a card and turn on auto-renewal, the amount for the next period is charged to that card automatically when the subscription ends.

5.2. You can turn auto-renewal off at any time with the "Active" switch on the **My businesses** screen or in **Billing and subscription**. The subscription then stays active until the end of the current paid period.

5.3. If your number of branches grows and the price rises significantly, auto-payment may be paused and you may be asked to confirm the new price manually.

5.4. If a charge fails, the subscription is paused and a notice is shown in the App.

## 6. Refunds

6.1. You get a full refund if:

- you were charged twice for the same period;
- you were charged because of a technical error but the subscription was not activated;
- the service was completely unavailable for more than 3 days through the Operator's fault - pro rata for the days it was down.

6.2. If you cancel a multi-month subscription (3, 6 or 12 months) early, we refund the months not yet started. The current month that has already started is not refunded.

6.3. A 1-month subscription is not refunded after activation; turning off auto-renewal stops the next charge.

6.4. If the account was blocked for a serious breach of the Terms, the payment may not be refunded.
