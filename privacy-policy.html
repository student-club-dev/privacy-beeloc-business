Beeloc Business - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what personal data the Beeloc Business mobile app (the "App") collects, why, and what choices you have. The App is made for business owners who publish their business, offers and job postings.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Beeloc ("we", "us"). You can reach us at quvonchbekgafurov07@gmail.com.

We do not collect payment card numbers in the App. We do not use advertising identifiers, third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not track you across other companies' apps and websites.

3. Location

The App asks for your location only while you are using it and only on the map screen. It is optional: you can always place the pin by moving the map. We do not track your location in the background. You can revoke the permission at any time in iOS Settings.

4. Photos

The App uses the system photo picker. It does not get access to your whole photo library, only to the pictures you select.

5. How we use data

  • Provide the service: account, businesses, listings, job postings, statistics and redemptions.
  • Verify your phone number and keep your account secure.
  • Review businesses and listings before they become visible to end users.
  • Answer support requests and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

6. What other people can see

The business name, type, branches, working hours, photos, prices and the contact phone number that you enter for a listing are visible to end users of our consumer app after approval. Your personal phone number, email and password are not shown in listings.

7. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. Data is processed only by:

  • our hosting and backend providers, to run the service;
  • an SMS provider, to deliver verification codes;
  • map services (OpenFreeMap / CARTO for map tiles and OpenStreetMap Nominatim for address search), which receive your IP address and the searched place or coordinates;
  • authorities, when the law requires it.

8. Security

All traffic between the App and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS. Your sign-in session token is stored in the iOS Keychain. Passwords are stored hashed. Access to data is limited to people who need it.

9. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account and associated data at any time from the Support page or by emailing quvonchbekgafurov07@gmail.com from the address or phone number of the account. We delete or anonymize the data within 30 days, except records we must keep by law.

10. Your rights

You can access, correct or delete your data, withdraw consent (for example location permission) and lodge a complaint with the competent authority. Contact us using the details above.

11. Children

The App is intended for business owners and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be shown in the App.
