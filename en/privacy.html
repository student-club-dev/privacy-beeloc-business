This policy also covers the Beeloc app for customers. Its specifics are described in section 2.6.

2.2. Payment data

Subscription payments are processed by Atmos, a licensed payment institution. Card details are sent to Atmos over a protected channel and processed by Atmos. We do not store your full card number or CVV. We keep only the card token issued by Atmos, the masked card number (e.g. 8600 •••• •••• 1234), and the amount, date and status of each payment.

2.3. Location

The App asks for your location only on the map screen, when you tap "My location", and only after you grant permission. It is optional: you can place the pin by moving the map. We do not track your location in the background and do not store it - only the branch coordinates you save are stored. You can revoke the permission at any time in your device settings.

2.4. Photos

The App uses the system photo picker. It does not get access to your whole photo library, only to the pictures you select.

2.5. Technical data

To run and secure the service: session token, device name, platform and app version (to show your active devices), request time, IP address and technical error records.

We do not use advertising identifiers or third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not track you across other companies' apps and websites.

2.6. The Beeloc app (for customers)

When you use the Beeloc app, we collect:

  • Phone number - to sign in and confirm it with an SMS code;
  • Name and profile photo (the photo is optional) - to show your profile;
  • Saved offers - to link them to your account and sync them across your devices;
  • Discount redemption records (which business, which offer, when) - so the business can confirm your discount code, and for statistics;
  • the technical data listed in section 2.5.

Recently viewed offers are stored only on your device so you can see them without an internet connection. The location and photo rules in sections 2.3 and 2.4 also apply to the Beeloc app.

4. What other people can see

After approval, your business name, type, branches, working hours, photos, prices, discount terms and the contact number you enter for a listing are publicly visible to users of the Beeloc app. Your personal phone number, email and password are never shown in listings.

5. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data or share it for advertising. Data is shared only as far as needed to provide the service:

  • Eskiz (Uzbekistan) - to deliver SMS codes: phone number and SMS text;
  • Atmos (Uzbekistan) - to accept payments and store your card: card details, payment amount;
  • Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany) - the hosting provider that runs our servers;
  • Map services - OpenFreeMap / CARTO (map tiles) and OpenStreetMap Nominatim (address search): they receive your IP address and the searched place or coordinates;
  • Competent authorities - only when the law requires it, in the legally prescribed manner.

6. Where data is stored

Data is stored on servers in a Hetzner Online GmbH data centre in Nuremberg, Germany. Germany is a member of the European Union, where strict personal data protection rules (GDPR) apply. By using the App, you agree that your data is stored and processed outside Uzbekistan in this way.

Map services are also located outside Uzbekistan; they receive only the minimum data needed to show the map and search addresses.

7. How long we keep data

  • Account data - while your account is active;
  • After the account is deleted - data is deleted or anonymised within 30 days;
  • Payment and accounting records - for the period required by law;
  • Technical logs - up to 90 days.

8. Your rights

  • Access - ask what data we hold about you;
  • Correction - fix wrong data in the App profile or by contacting us;
  • Deletion - delete your account and related data;
  • Withdraw consent - for example, revoke the location permission;
  • Complaint - contact the competent state authority.

We answer requests within 10 business days.

Deleting your account

In the App: Settings → Delete account. An SMS code is sent to your account's verified number; after you enter it, your account, businesses, listings, job postings, photos and saved cards are deleted. If you cannot sign in, email quvonchbekgafurov07@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account" and the account's phone number. Deletion cannot be undone.

To delete your Beeloc app account, you can also email this address with the account's phone number.

9. Security

We protect your data with the following measures:

  • All traffic between the App and our servers is encrypted with HTTPS, and the App additionally verifies the server certificate (certificate pinning);
  • Passwords are never stored in plain text - only as a one-way hash;
  • Your sign-in token is stored encrypted in the device's secure storage (Android Keystore, iOS Keychain);
  • The local cache database on your device is encrypted; signing out clears the token and the cache;
  • Important actions (sign up, password reset, account deletion) are confirmed with an SMS code;
  • The payment page opens in a secure browser window, not in an embedded WebView;
  • Staff access to data is limited to what their role requires.

No service can guarantee 100% security, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures. If a data breach is detected, we will notify you and the competent authority as required by law.

If you find a security vulnerability, please report it to quvonchbekgafurov07@gmail.com.
